Security Tips
  • Your account can be accessed from multiple devices simultaneously.
  • Regularly review active devices to ensure only authorized devices are connected.
  • If you notice an unfamiliar device, click "Revoke Access" to disconnect it immediately.
  • Use strong, unique passwords and enable device notifications for added security.
  • Logging out from a device will disconnect only that device from your account.
